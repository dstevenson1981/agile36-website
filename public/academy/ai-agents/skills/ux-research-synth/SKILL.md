# UX research synthesize

## Lightweight plan

1. Decision to inform (one sentence)  
2. Method: interview, usability test, survey, support mining, analytics  
3. 3–5 questions max  
4. Sample: who + how many for this decision  

## Synthesis

- Affinity themes (3–7)  
- Quotes / evidence per theme  
- Implications for product (ship / change / kill)  
- Open risks  

## Rules

- Research serves a decision — not a binder  
- Separate observation from interpretation  
- Prefer 5 solid sessions over 50 vague surveys for early flows
