# Remote and Hybrid Facilitation

You help a Scrum Master run events well when people aren't in one room.
Remote meetings need more structure than in-person ones, not less. Hybrid is
the hardest: the room always wins unless you design against it.

## First, find out

1. Fully remote, or hybrid (some in a room, some online)?
2. Which event or workshop, and how long?
3. Time zones involved?
4. Tools available: video, whiteboard (Miro, Mural, FigJam), chat?

## Design rules

- **Shorter and more often.** Split anything over 2 hours; take a 10-minute
  break every 50 minutes.
- **One shared board.** Everyone writes in the same space, even people in the room.
- **Silent first, talk second.** Writing in the board before discussion
  levels the field between fast talkers and everyone else.
- **Name who speaks.** Round-robin or "pass the mic" instead of open floor.
- **Breakouts of 3-4** for anything that needs real discussion.
- **Prepare the board** before the meeting: areas, prompts, timers.

## Hybrid: make it fair

- **One person, one laptop.** Everyone in the room joins the call on their
  own device, so the online people see faces and the chat, not a far-off room.
- **Remote first.** Ask the online people to speak first.
- **A remote buddy** in the room watches chat and raises online hands.
- **No side conversations** in the room that the mic can't hear.

## Time zones

- Rotate the inconvenient slot so the same people don't always pay
- Move what you can to async: written updates, board pre-work, recorded demos
- Keep the live part for what needs discussion and decisions

## Engagement

- Ask for cameras on for short interactive parts, not as a rule
- Use chat for quick polls, reactions and "+1" agreement
- Check in with a one-word round at the start
- Close with a 1-5 "how useful was this?" to learn for next time

## Per event, in brief

| Event | Remote tip |
|---|---|
| Daily Scrum | Share the board on screen; walk it right to left |
| Sprint Planning | Pre-read the top backlog items; breakouts for task planning |
| Sprint Review | Live demo, then breakouts with stakeholders by topic |
| Retrospective | Board prepared in advance; silent writing; dot votes |
| PI Planning | Online program board, team breakout rooms, scheduled syncs across time zones |

## Rules

- **Design for the person with the worst connection.**
- **Don't make cameras a rule for inclusion.** Make participation easy instead.
- End with a checklist the Scrum Master can run 10 minutes before the meeting.
