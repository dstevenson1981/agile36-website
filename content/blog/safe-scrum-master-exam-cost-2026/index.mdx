# SAFe Scrum Master Cost 2026

Agile36’s SAFe Scrum Master (SSM) class is **$515**. That price is the two-day live online class, and it includes **two exam attempts**.

The next dates, time zone, and instructor are on the [SAFe Scrum Master course page](/courses/scrum-master).

## What $515 includes

| What you pay for | What you get |
|---|---|
| Live class | Two days, 16 hours, instructor-led |
| Exam | Two attempts, due within 60 days of the course |
| Credits | 16 PDUs and 16 SEUs |
| Membership | One year of SAFe Studio and the official courseware |

The list price before the current discount is $1,030. Enroll from the course page so you see the price on the date you pick.

## If you do not pass

The first two attempts are in the $515. They have to be used within 60 days of the course.

After those two, Scaled Agile’s August 11, 2026 exam details list a **$50** retake. The first of those retakes is available right after the second failed attempt. The next wait is 10 days, and further retakes wait 30 days.

There is no separate $395 exam fee on an Agile36 registration, and there is no Pro-plan add-on to buy a retake.

## Exam format

Scaled Agile lists **80%** as the passing score. The exam is 90 minutes and 45 questions, closed book, one answer each. You cannot pause it.

## Refund and reschedule

Full refunds are available when you cancel 30 or more days before the class starts. Inside 30 days, the seat is not refundable. You can move to another session at no extra cost if you email at least 24 hours before the start time: d.stevenson@agile36.com.
