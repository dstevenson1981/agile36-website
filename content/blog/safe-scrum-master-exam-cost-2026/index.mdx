### Training Course Investment

The exam fee is separate from training costs, which typically range from $1,295 to $1,795 depending on the provider. At Agile36, our SSM course includes:

### Hidden Costs to Consider

**Exam Retakes**: If you don't pass on the first attempt, each retake costs another $395. In my experience training thousands of SSM candidates, about 15% need a second attempt.

**Annual Renewal**: After your first year, maintaining certification requires:
- Continuing education credits
- Annual renewal fee (typically $100-150)
- Proof of SAFe practice in your role

The SSM exam costs $395 in 2026, unchanged from 2024-2025. This fee is set by Scaled Agile and consistent globally.
