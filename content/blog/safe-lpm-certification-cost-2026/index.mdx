## FAQ

### How much does SAFe LPM certification cost in total?

The complete SAFe LPM certification typically costs $2,940-$4,395 for the first year, including training ($2,195-$3,500), exam fee ($595), and study materials ($150-$300). Annual renewal costs $100 plus continuing education expenses.

### Is SAFe LPM certification worth the investment?

Yes, for qualified professionals. LPM certification typically delivers 15-30% salary increases within 18 months, with most candidates achieving ROI in 1-3 months. The certification is particularly valuable for portfolio management and senior transformation roles.

### Can my employer pay for SAFe LPM certification?

Most Fortune 500 companies (78%) reimburse SAFe LPM training costs through professional development budgets. The certification is often classified as strategic skill development with high business value.

### How does LPM certification cost compare to other SAFe certifications?

LPM exam costs $595 compared to SA ($795), RTE ($595), and SPC ($795). However, LPM training is typically 3 days versus 2 days for most other certifications, making the total investment similar to premium SAFe certifications.

### What's included in the SAFe LPM exam fee?

The $595 exam fee includes the 2-hour, 60-question certification exam with one attempt. It also provides one year of certification status and access to Scaled Agile's LPM community platform.

### Are there financing options for SAFe LPM certification?

Many training providers offer payment plans or corporate billing. Some professionals use professional development loans or credit cards with promotional rates. Corporate sponsorship remains the most common funding method.

### How often do SAFe LPM certification costs increase?

Scaled Agile typically increases exam fees annually by 5-11%. Training costs vary by provider but generally follow market inflation rates of 3-8% per year.
