# SAFe POPM Certification Cost 2026

Agile36’s SAFe Product Owner / Product Manager (POPM) class is **$545**. That price is the two-day live online class, and it includes **two exam attempts**.

The next dates are on the [POPM course page](/courses/product-owner-manager).

## What $545 includes

| What you pay for | What you get |
|---|---|
| Live class | Two days, 16 hours, instructor-led |
| Exam | Two attempts, due within 60 days of the course |
| Credits | 16 PDUs and 16 SEUs |
| Membership | One year of SAFe Studio and the official courseware |

The list price before the current discount is $1,090.

## If you do not pass

The first two attempts are in the $545. They have to be used within 60 days of the course.

After those two, Scaled Agile’s May 5, 2026 exam details list a **$50** retake. The first of those retakes is available right after the second failed attempt. The next wait is 10 days, and further retakes wait 30 days.

There is no separate $395 exam fee on an Agile36 registration, and a retake is not sold as a $77 add-on.

## Exam format

Scaled Agile lists **82%** as the passing score. The exam is 90 minutes and 45 questions, closed book, one answer each.

## Refund and reschedule

Full refunds are available when you cancel 30 or more days before the class starts. Inside 30 days, the seat is not refundable. You can move to another session at no extra cost if you email at least 24 hours before the start time: d.stevenson@agile36.com.
