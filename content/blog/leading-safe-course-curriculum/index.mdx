# Leading SAFe Course Curriculum: What You Actually Learn (2026 Guide)

**Leading SAFe is a 2-day course that teaches leaders the full Scaled Agile Framework: Lean-Agile principles and leadership, building Agile Release Trains, planning with PI Planning, Lean Portfolio Management, and leading the transformation itself — finishing with the SAFe Agilist (SA) certification exam.** It's the framework's flagship course, and the curriculum is why: it's the one place the entire system is taught end-to-end.

Here's what each part of the curriculum actually covers, so you know what you're signing up for before you book a seat in a [live Leading SAFe cohort](/courses/leading-safe).

## The Curriculum at a Glance

| Module | What You Learn |
|--------|----------------|
| **1. Thriving in the Digital Age** | Why business agility matters; the case for SAFe; the seven core competencies |
| **2. Lean-Agile Leadership** | The mindset: Lean thinking, the SAFe principles, leading by example |
| **3. Team and Technical Agility** | How [Agile teams and teams-of-teams](/blog/what-is-an-agile-release-train) organize and deliver |
| **4. Agile Product Delivery** | Customer centricity, [PI Planning](/blog/what-is-pi-planning), DevOps, releasing on demand |
| **5. Lean Portfolio Management** | [Funding value streams, portfolio Kanban, lean governance](/blog/lean-portfolio-management) |
| **6. Leading the Change** | Implementation roadmap: launching your first ART and scaling from there |

## Module by Module

### 1. Thriving in the Digital Age with Business Agility

The "why" module. You'll cover why traditional management struggles with software-driven markets, and how SAFe's seven core competencies fit together as an operating system for the enterprise. If you've only seen [SAFe's big picture diagram](/blog/what-is-safe-framework), this is where it starts making sense as a system.

### 2. Becoming a Lean-Agile Leader

The mindset module — and the exam's favorite. You'll work through the Lean-Agile mindset, the SAFe principles (economics, systems thinking, decentralized decision-making), and what "leading by example" concretely means during a transformation. Expect several [exam questions](/blog/leading-safe-exam-questions-2026) drawn from the principles.

### 3. Establishing Team and Technical Agility

How the delivery engine is built: cross-functional Agile teams, the team-of-teams structure of the [Agile Release Train](/blog/what-is-an-agile-release-train), and built-in quality practices that keep a 100-person train from producing a 100-person mess.

### 4. Building Solutions with Agile Product Delivery

The delivery module: customer centricity and design thinking, prioritizing with WSJF economics, the PI Planning cadence that synchronizes every team, and the DevOps pipeline that lets the business release on demand rather than on the calendar.

### 5. Exploring Lean Portfolio Management

The money module — usually the biggest eye-opener for executives. Traditional annual project budgeting gives way to [funding value streams](/blog/lean-portfolio-management), portfolio Kanban, and lightweight governance. Leaders continuing down this track often follow with the dedicated [LPM certification](/blog/safe-lpm-certification-cost-2026).

### 6. Leading the Change

The roadmap module: SAFe's implementation sequence — train leaders, identify value streams, launch your first ART, expand — and your role in making it stick. This is where the course turns from framework knowledge into a transformation plan you can start executing.

The curriculum assumes leadership altitude, not team mechanics: managers, executives, program and project managers, architects, and consultants. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long is the Leading SAFe course?**
Two days (16 hours), live and instructor-led. [Agile36 cohorts](/courses/leading-safe) run weekday and weekend schedules, taught by certified SPCs.

**Do I need prerequisites?**
No formal prerequisites. Experience with Agile concepts helps, but the course teaches the framework from first principles.

**Is the curriculum the same at every training provider?**
The courseware is standardized by Scaled Agile — every partner teaches the same modules. What differs is the instructor's real transformation experience and class format, which is where [live SPC-taught cohorts](/courses/leading-safe) earn their keep.
