# SAFe for Teams Certification Cost 2026: SP Pricing Guide

**SAFe for Teams certification costs $599 at Agile36 in 2026 — a live 2-day course taught by certified SPCs, with your SAFe Practitioner (SP) exam attempt included.** Many providers price the same certification anywhere from the high hundreds into four figures, so it's worth knowing exactly what you're paying for.

Here's the complete cost picture: what's included, ongoing renewal costs, and how teams save when certifying together.

## Quick Facts: SAFe for Teams (SP) Costs

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Agile36 Price (2026)** | [$599, exam included](/courses/safe-for-teams) |
| **Course Length** | 2 days (16 hours), live online |
| **Exam** | First attempt included; retakes $50 |
| **PDUs / SEUs** | 16 |
| **Certification Validity** | 1 year |
| **Renewal Fee** | $100 + 10 SAFe PDUs |

## What the $599 Includes

- **The live course** — 2 days taught by a certified SAFe Program Consultant (SPC), weekday and weekend cohort options
- **Official SAFe courseware** and a practice test from Scaled Agile
- **The SAFe Practitioner (SP) exam** — your first attempt, with a 30-day window after the course ends
- **One year of SAFe Community Platform access** once certified
- **16 PDUs/SEUs** toward PMI and Scrum Alliance renewals

There is no separate exam fee to budget for — the exam is part of course registration. If you need a retake, Scaled Agile charges $50 after a 10-day wait. Preparing already? Work through our free [SAFe for Teams exam questions](/blog/safe-for-teams-exam-questions-2026).

## Ongoing Costs After Certification

**Annual renewal: $100 + 10 PDUs.** Like all SAFe certifications, SP renews yearly. The 10 PDUs can come from courses, webinars, and community activity — many practitioners earn them through work they're already doing.

**Moving up the ladder.** Team members often follow SP with a role certification — [SAFe Scrum Master](/blog/safe-scrum-master-exam-cost-2026), POPM, or eventually [Leading SAFe](/blog/leading-safe-certification-cost-2026) for leadership tracks. Budget for one step up within 12–18 months if you're building a SAFe career.

## Who Pays: Individuals vs. Teams

SAFe for Teams is unusual among SAFe certifications: it's designed to be taken by **whole teams**, often as part of an ART launch. That changes the economics.

**If your company is launching an Agile Release Train**, training is usually budgeted as part of the transformation — ask your leadership before paying out of pocket. Training every member of an [Agile Release Train](/blog/what-is-an-agile-release-train) is the standard SAFe implementation pattern.

**Corporate and group pricing.** Certifying a team together costs less per person and gets everyone the same working vocabulary at once. For team quotes, see our [corporate training](/corporate) options.

**If you're paying yourself**, SP is one of the most affordable entry points into SAFe — cheaper than the role-specific certifications, and a legitimate resume credential for developers, testers, BAs, and other team members working in (or interviewing at) SAFe shops.

## Is SAFe for Teams Certification Worth the Cost?

**Worth it if:** you work on (or are joining) an Agile Release Train, your organization runs SAFe, or you're a team-level contributor who wants SAFe vocabulary and a credential without committing to a role-track certification.

**Consider a different certification if:** you're a Scrum Master, Product Owner, or leader — the role tracks ([SSM](/blog/safe-scrum-master-exam-cost-2026), POPM, [Leading SAFe](/blog/leading-safe-certification-cost-2026)) are better matched and similarly priced. ## FAQ

### How much does SAFe for Teams certification cost in total?

At Agile36, [$599 total](/courses/safe-for-teams) — live course, official courseware, practice test, and the SP exam attempt are all included. The only later cost is the $100 annual renewal (plus 10 PDUs).

### Is the SP exam included, or do I pay separately?

Included. Your first attempt comes with course registration and your exam window stays open for 30 days after the course. Retakes are $50.

### Is there a way to take the SP exam without the course?

No — SAFe certifications require completing the corresponding course through a Scaled Agile partner. That's why comparing course prices is the real cost comparison.

### Does the certification expire?

SP is valid for one year and renews for $100 + 10 PDUs, the same renewal model as other SAFe certifications.
