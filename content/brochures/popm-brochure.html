Scaled Agile's 2026.05 release threads AI through every lesson rather than bolting it on at the end: AI-supported customer research, generative AI for features and iteration goals, prompting techniques you'll actually practise, and a clear-eyed treatment of responsible AI. You leave able to pass the exam — and able to use these tools on Monday.

AI-Empowered SAFe® POPM AI for product roles
07 · AI for Product Owners & Product Managers

What you'll actually
practise with AI

Four hands-on activities, spread across the two days. You write the prompt, read what comes back critically, and edit it into something a real Agile Release Train could use.

Develop your SAFe message Lesson 1

Start with basic prompting: goal, role, task, context, details. Learn what a prompt actually is, and why the same question phrased two ways gives you two very different answers.

Prompt, refine, and edit Lesson 2

Use AI-supported research to explore customer needs, then draft customer-centric features — and practise spotting where the model has confidently invented something.

AI-empowered PI Planning Lesson 3

Work through where AI genuinely helps in planning — and where structure, not speed, is what liberates a room full of teams.

AI-assisted Iteration Planning Lesson 4

Give a model a set of user stories and have it synthesize two or three outcome-focused iteration goals — not one goal per story. Then edit for the business outcome the team is actually chasing.

Prompting techniques you'll take away
  • Instruction — ask for the steps to achieve an objective
  • Analysis — hand over data and let the model surface patterns and trends
  • Chain-of-thought — ask for the reasoning, not just the answer
  • Retrieval-augmented generation — ground answers in your own documents
  • Knowing when you need an agent — and when you plainly don't
Responsible AI

A full topic, not a disclaimer. You'll work through common AI risks and the three aspects of responsible use:

  • Trustworthy AI — reliable enough to base a decision on
  • Explainable AI — you can say why it produced that
  • Human-centric AI — the person stays accountable
